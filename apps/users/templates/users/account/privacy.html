Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This explains what Music Luvers collects, why, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). The data controller is Music Luvers (privacy@musicluvers.site).

What we collect

  • Account: first name, email, password (stored scrambled; we can't read it).
  • Profile: gender, who you want to see, birthday (we show your age, not the date) and photos.
  • Location: your phone's position when you tap "Share my location" or open the app with location allowed. We round it to about 1 km before saving and never store your exact position. We look up your city name with OpenStreetMap.
  • Music: genres, artists and songs you pick, and your Last.fm listening if you connect it.
  • Activity: likes and passes, matches, messages, reports and blocks.
  • Technical: IP address and browser type, for security (for example, to stop password-guessing).
  • App usage: which pages you open and steps you take (for example "signed up", "liked", "sent a message"), your device type and rough country, so we can see where the app is confusing and fix it. This never includes your messages, what you type, or your password.

Why we use it

  • To run the service: find people whose music and location fit yours, show your profile, deliver messages and notifications (performance of our agreement with you).
  • To keep people safe: review reports, stop spam, fake accounts and abuse (legitimate interest and legal obligation).
  • To send account emails: confirming your email and resetting your password.
  • To improve the app: see which screens people use and where they get stuck (legitimate interest).

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't show it to advertisers.

Who can see what

Other users see your first name, age, photos, city, rough distance ("8 km away"), music and song answers. They never see your email, birthday, exact location or who you've blocked or reported.

Services we use

Hosting and database (Neon), app usage statistics (PostHog), photo storage (Cloudinary), email (Resend), city lookup (OpenStreetMap Nominatim), music search (Spotify and Last.fm). Some of these store data outside Nigeria; we only use providers that protect it with appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

While your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, location, music, matches and messages straight away. We may keep a report you were the subject of for up to 12 months, for safety.

Your rights

You can see and change your details in Profile, and delete your account in Profile → Delete my account. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to stop a use of it, or to delete it, by emailing privacy@musicluvers.site. You can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Age

Music Luvers is only for people 18 and over. If we learn someone is under 18, we delete their account.

Changes

We'll update the date at the top when this changes, and tell you in the app about important changes.
